STEP 1 OF YOUR PATH TO PROPERTY OWNERSHIP

Get Pre-Qualified Before You Start Looking

One of the smartest first steps in buying property is understanding what comfortably fits your budget before you begin viewing properties.

Whether you're purchasing your first home, buying land, investing, relocating, or searching for a vacation property in The Bahamas, getting pre-qualified gives you a clearer understanding of your options and allows you to move forward with confidence.

Learn How It Works Ask Ruby a Question

Why Get Pre-Qualified?

Getting pre-qualified isn't about committing to a mortgage—it's about gaining clarity before making one of life's biggest financial decisions.

Understand Your Budget

Knowing what comfortably fits your financial circumstances helps you focus on properties that are both realistic and affordable.

Shop With Confidence

Instead of wondering whether a property is within reach, you'll have a clearer understanding of the price range that makes sense for you.

Be Better Prepared

Understanding your financing options early helps you make informed decisions and move forward with greater confidence when the right property comes along.
