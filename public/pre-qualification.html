Why Get Pre-Qualified?

Getting pre-qualified isn't about committing to a mortgage—it's about gaining clarity before making one of life's biggest financial decisions.

Understand Your Budget

Knowing what comfortably fits your financial circumstances helps you focus on properties that are both realistic and affordable.

Shop With Confidence

Instead of wondering whether a property is within reach, you'll have a clearer understanding of the price range that makes sense for you.

Be Better Prepared

Understanding your financing options early helps you make informed decisions and move forward with greater confidence when the right property comes along.

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to some of the questions I'm most often asked before buyers begin their property search.

Do I need to be pre-qualified before looking at properties?

Not necessarily—but it's strongly recommended. Understanding your financial position early helps you focus on suitable properties and avoid disappointment later.

Does getting pre-qualified mean I have to use a particular lender?

No. Getting pre-qualified simply helps you better understand your financing options. You're free to choose the lender that best meets your needs.

What information will a lender usually ask for?

Requirements vary, but lenders commonly request proof of income, identification, employment details, information about your assets and liabilities, and other supporting financial documents.

Can I still buy property if I'm self-employed?

Yes. Many self-employed buyers successfully purchase property. The documentation required may differ, so it's helpful to discuss your individual circumstances with a qualified mortgage professional.
